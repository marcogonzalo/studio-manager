A professional interior design budget organises **services**, **products by space** and **taxes**, and reaches the client as a clear PDF. In Veta that data lives in the project: the PDF is **generated** from it — you do not fill a blank downloaded template.

## What the budget module solves

1. **Structure by space** — living room, kitchen or bedroom products without duplicated sheets.
2. **Services by phase** — design fees, site supervision and procurement.
3. **Reusable catalog** — same products and suppliers across jobs.
4. **PDF from live data** — one click reflects current prices and quantities.
5. **Internal costs hidden from the client** — the PDF shows sale price, not your margin.

## From line item to document

Typical flow:

1. Define spaces and add products (from catalog or new).
2. Add budget lines (construction, fees, external, operations).
3. Configure tax (single rate or multitax by plan).
4. Generate the client PDF.

Structure and best practices: [budget PDF template](/en/interior-design-budget-pdf-template).

Once the budget is in Veta, [cost control](/en/cost-control-profitability-interior-design) compares estimated vs actual on the same lines, and [purchase orders](/en/purchase-orders-suppliers-interior-design) group budget products.
