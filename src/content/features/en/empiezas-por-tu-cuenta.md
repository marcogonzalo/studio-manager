When you start on your own, the job is not “running a studio”—it is finishing a first commission well. You need to know what you promised the client, what it really costs, and what is left to order. Veta organises that work in a single project; the [Base plan](/en/base-plan-first-interior-design-project) is the plan for that stage.

## What the work looks like when you start

On a first project you usually do everything yourself:

1. **Capture the brief** somewhere that is not the chat thread.
2. **Build the budget by space** (living room, kitchen, bath) without rebuilding a spreadsheet each time.
3. **Track suppliers and lead times** so a critical order is not forgotten.
4. **Share progress** with the client without exposing your margins.

Without a dedicated tool, that spills across sheets, screenshots, and WhatsApp. One active project is enough: you do not need roles, white-label PDFs, or five projects at once.

## What Veta does at this stage

- One **project** with client, spaces, and line items.
- A **PDF budget** generated from project data (not a downloadable blank template).
- **Visible costs** so you can see whether the job holds.
- A **client view** with a read-only link.

Limits and pricing live on the [Base plan](/en/base-plan-first-interior-design-project) page. This page does not replace that sheet—it tells you whether you are in this stage.

## When you are no longer “starting out”

When a second or third client arrives at the same time, Base gets tight. Move to [Freelancers and independents](/en/app-freelancer-multiple-projects-interior-design) and the [Pro plan](/en/pro-plan-for-independent-interior-designers). If you already work with people on site and several projects at once, see [Studios with a team](/en/studios-with-team-interior-design-project-management).
