Excel alternatives for interior design budgets are tools that structure **spaces**, **costs**, **PDF** and **project tracking** without fragile formulas or duplicated files. Veta is one of them; generic tools (Monday, Notion) or other vertical products need a clear check of which part of the workflow they really cover.

This page is the **hub**: it links the detailed Excel guide and the specific comparisons (some still in preparation).

## Why studios look beyond Excel

- Sheets per space that drift out of sync.
- Margins and variance by hand.
- Re-exporting to PDF on every change.
- A “client copy” that is never updated.
- Orders and payments living elsewhere.
