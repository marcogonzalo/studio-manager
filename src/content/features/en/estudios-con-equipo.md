A studio with a team rarely fails for lack of talent—it fails when direction and site do not see the same number. Elena needs to know whether the project makes money; Javi needs orders and lead times without asking three times. Veta keeps that thread on the project; the [Studio plan](/en/studio-plan-for-architecture-and-interior-design-firms) is the plan for that stage.

## What the work looks like in a studio with a team

1. **Several active projects** with different clients and phases.
2. **Budgets with the studio’s brand**, not generic layouts.
3. **Purchase orders and suppliers** tied to what was budgeted, so site does not improvise.
4. **Costs and payments** visible to direction without rebuilding sheets.
5. A **clean client view** to share progress without internal costs.

The pain is not “one more feature”—it is that each person works in their own file. The tool has to be the same place for everyone.

## What Veta adds (without repeating the pricing sheet)

- Parallel projects with margin control.
- Purchase orders and payment coverage linked to the budget.
- Studio-branded PDF (white label) when the presentation matters.
- Public client view without accounts or sensitive data.

Limits, pricing, and comparisons live on the [Studio plan](/en/studio-plan-for-architecture-and-interior-design-firms) page.
